## What we can reasonably attempt

The highest useful target is improved action-control/distribution quality per measured
compute on a frozen tabletop task, against well-tuned CVAE/numeric diffusion and one
faithfully documented external-method adaptation. Matching information is essential:
more observed frames, oracle physics, future tracks or richer control prompts change the task.

The initial-image uncertainty problem remains distinct from identifying physical parameters
from an observed interaction. A generated distribution can be well calibrated without
knowing which hidden mass was used for one realized reference rollout.

Our recommended starting backbone is an engineering inference from current code,
deadline and task geometry. The literature does not establish that this particular
one-million-parameter U-Net will outperform latent or object-centric alternatives.

For uncertainty evaluation, Gneiting and Raftery's proper-scoring-rule framework
supports using a distribution score such as the energy score alongside coverage,
plausibility and tracking failures. It does not validate our tracker or a particular
finite-sample implementation. [Primary publication](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.1198/016214506000001437).
