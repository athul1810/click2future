## A short explanation for the group

We have a small tabletop, one selected object, and a specified push. We want a learned
model to generate what happens next, including the other object's response to a collision.
The simulator gives us labels for checking that behavior, but its playback is not our
model output. We will compare a VAE with diffusion, then compare global and localized
action representations while keeping the information and model size the same.

The first target is action-responsive video on held-out scenes. After that, we can
ask whether extra physics supervision helps and whether different samples represent
the uncertainty from hidden mass/friction. We will profile the actual assigned GPU
before promising training hours. Our local untrained architecture and gradients work;
the dataset/sampler/trainer and allocated TC2 GPU validation still need to be completed.
