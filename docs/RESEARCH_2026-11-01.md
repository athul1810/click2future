snapshot. The next bounded prior-only screen is #73 and has no quality result yet.

this is a targeted primary-source review through 1 november, not a proof that
every relevant paper has been found. reported benchmark numbers belong to their
own protocols. no external checkpoint has been independently reproduced here.
an unavailable model, an impressive gallery and a result with privileged inputs
cannot supply a matched comparison.

Physics-IQ's [official benchmark](https://physics-iq.github.io/) distinguishes
visual realism from physical understanding. its categories and input protocols
differ from our numeric-action tabletop experiment. leaderboard snapshots must
retain benchmark version, date, input conditions and compute; no cross-benchmark
score conversion is meaningful here.

## what the failure pattern suggests

1. **information:** one resting image cannot distinguish independently drawn
   hidden masses with identical pixels. model a conditional distribution; do not
   claim system identification without an observed response. pair the sampled
   world across action interventions and keep all four seeds.
2. **representation:** E7 messages use object descriptors, centres and bounding
   gaps. detailed hulls are present in the training loss but not the learned
   message encoder. local surfaces could help contact timing and rotation. that
   is a testable engineering inference, not a conclusion from RiCo's results.
3. **training:** E8 froze a teacher and changed it by only 0.0055 pixels RMS.
   that does not rule out temporal models generally. full refinement plus a
   parameter-matched pooled-surface control is a different experiment.
4. **coverage:** #68 separates old visible scenes from a larger independent
   visible distribution at matched example count. it also changes layout and
   actual contact prevalence, so it cannot isolate geometry count alone.
5. **measurement:** low ADE or high SSIM can reward slow/static copying. require
   force scaling, selected-object isolation, timing, penetration depth/duration,
   missing-track accounting, rest and multireference coverage together. contact
   can be plausible while differing from one realized hidden-world reference.
6. **demo:** our sprites are 64px observations. polishing the UI or replacing
   sprites with photorealistic assets will not fix contact. show the input,
   selected mask and force, every declared future, a same-world action change,
   measured latency and a failure view. keep references separate from inference.

## 1 november experiment order (historical)

- finish #68 collection and exact replay, including Ananya's requested full-clip
  selection drift and paired force scaling. `submission/trajectory_response.py`
  adds a supplementary probe without editing historical evaluator sources.
- prototype learned local-surface messages using only observed silhouettes and
  predicted poses. use a pooled-surface control with identical learned parameters;
  both start at zero residual around identical E7 weights. train the whole model,
  rather than another frozen-teacher residual. no solver/contact projection,
  seed selection or zero-force override.
- freeze the cache after reading #68's outcome. compare fixed final updates with
  equal batches, loss, noise draws, parameter counts and training budget. old
  quality failures remain failures; keep every training attempt.
- stop expanding architecture after 7 november. from 8-10 november, repeat only
  a useful candidate with independent training seeds and a newly frozen holdout;
  otherwise retain the best-supported existing demonstration and explain limits.
  finish report figures, contribution reviews and rehearsal by 12 november, then
  freeze the deliverables for 14 november. these are proposed internal gates, not
  changes to the course upload cutoff.

an honest improvement target is fewer severe contacts and correct action
responses at unchanged completeness/spread/compute on this declared task. it
would still be a local result until a faithful external adaptation is evaluated
with the same information and protocol.
