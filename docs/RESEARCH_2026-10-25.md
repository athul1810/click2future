# research and next experiment, 25 october

we have not demonstrated a win over external state of the art. our strongest
comparison is still against our own frozen controls. the current wall model
tracks 95/96 complete validation clips but penetrates in 15/96. its four-scene,
one-training-seed result does not establish generalization. the source review
also includes the merged action-horizon scorer and ananya's three flow-cvae seeds.

## primary-source landscape

these are different tasks. published scores are not a common leaderboard.

| work | relevant mechanism | comparison limit and implication |
|---|---|---|
| [StreamForce](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.07508) and [code](https://github.com/neu-vi/StreamForce) | causal video generation with changing local/global forces | substantially broader visuals and interaction than our fixed 0.25-second push. upstream reports about 40 GB demo VRAM without captioning and assumes eight GPUs for training. weights are not tested here; units and metrics are unmatched. |
| [PhysCtrl](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.20358) and [code](https://github.com/cwchenwang/physctrl) | diffusion over 3d particle motion, physical conditioning and subsequent video synthesis | supports several materials and uses 550k synthetic animations. physical properties are supplied, whereas ours are hidden. adopt the separation of dynamics and appearance, without claiming equivalent coverage. |
| [PhyCo](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.28169) | physical property maps, pretrained video adaptation and physics-focused reward feedback | physical controls and broad video priors change the information and compute budget. useful evidence for training physical behaviour directly; no matched reproduction here. |
| [PhyParam](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.18924) | instance-specific routing of force and physical attributes; motion-focused supervision | explicit masses/friction/restitution differ from our latent-world task. supports keeping action attached to the selected instance rather than global appearance alone. |
| [PhysGen](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.18964) | image understanding, a physical dynamics engine, then generative rendering | demonstrates a strong hybrid alternative. an inference-time engine would be a separately labelled method, not a hidden replacement for our learned motion. |
| [PhysDreamer](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.13026) | distils video priors into interactive dynamics of reconstructed objects | different reconstruction/optimization setup. useful for a later image-to-interaction pipeline, not an off-the-shelf score comparison. |
| [GNS](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v119/sanchez-gonzalez20a.html) | learned interaction graphs and rollout training | assumes physical state/history rather than our observable image interface. motivates a stateful interaction model if the current trajectory representation plateaus. |
| [learned rigid contact](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v205/allen23a.html) and [FIGNet](https://openreview.net/pdf?id=J7Uh781A05p) | graph representations targeted at discontinuous contact and surface interaction | contact deserves explicit relational representation. neither paper proves that adding an attention block to our small corpus will work. |
| [CoPhy](https://arxiv.org/abs/1909.12000) | counterfactual physical prediction and shared hidden causes | motivates testing the same sampled world under changed actions. its observation and intervention protocol differs from a single resting image. |
| [Physics-IQ](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.09038) and [Physics-IQ Verified](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.18943) | physical evaluation beyond visual quality; audited reference validity | benchmark design can change conclusions. retain oracle calibration, missing outcomes and all action families, rather than maximize one raw score. |
| [EDM](https://github.com/NVlabs/edm) | preconditioning and numerical diffusion sampling | the existing 16-step Heun sampler stays frozen for the next comparison. changing it would confound the loss experiment. |

## what the problem actually permits

1. **identifiability.** simulator appearance is sampled independently of hidden
   mass/contact coefficients. a resting image cannot reveal those exact values.
   predict a conditional distribution; do not promise to recover the hidden world.
2. **causal coverage.** the original training data associates selecting the second
   object with collision. balanced paired actions address this; ananya independently
   found the same confound in flow-cvae. additional training examples must remain
   in training worlds, with final suites reserved.
3. **geometry.** accurate colour masks and nearly complete tracks can coexist with
   sustained penetration. keep the pre-compositing hull diagnostic alongside RGB.
4. **uncertainty.** a different seed is not proof of a plausible physical world.
   forward spread remains too small in our wall model. inspect coverage and shared
   action responses, not RGB diversity alone.
5. **measurement.** a correct alternative physical reference has exact-label event
   f1 0.2368 against the first reference. that ambiguity is distinct from sustained
   overlap. the full-horizon scorer supplements the unchanged early window.
6. **data scale.** the action supplement expands actions, not the 16 training
   geometries. external methods' much broader scene coverage is not a fair claim
   we can inherit from a better-looking demo.
7. **compute and delivery.** prioritize one bounded causal comparison on the
   available GPU. retain the required cvae/diffusion comparison, repeats and final
   test freeze. a large video model download does not establish a usable baseline.

## selected implementation and architecture ceiling

retain the observable-slot extraction, joint object/time EDM, rigid observed-pixel
renderer and current inference. the next change is a training objective aimed at
rare deep contact failures. it is not a new architecture or a novelty claim.

if that plateaus, the next architecture to investigate is a conditional shared
world latent plus stateful object-contact message passing, followed by joint
SE(2) trajectories and the same rigid renderer. a latent must be shared across
counterfactual actions, with no future labels or simulator calls at inference.
physical states/poses can supervise training, explicitly separate from E0/E2/E3.
that larger branch needs train-only experiments proving useful uncertainty and
stable rollout before any main comparison. it is not implemented in this change.

## predeclared contact-risk experiment

the old penetration term averages squared excess over all frames and examples.
rare violations can contribute little despite being obvious in a clip. this is a
hypothesis about optimization, not proof of the remaining model's failure cause.

add a training-only upper-tail mean: square depth beyond the unchanged one-pixel
margin, take the worst ceil(0.25*T) frames per clip, apply the existing low-noise
gate, then take the worst ceil(0.25*B) clips. multiply by 256. the existing mean
loss stays. this is inspired by [tail-risk optimization](https://sites.math.washington.edu/~rtr/papers/rtr179-CVaR1.pdf),
with explicit rounding; no exact CVaR mass or probabilistic safety guarantee is
claimed. a sparse unit fixture receives 16 times the original mean gradient,
while feasible labels and high-noise rows receive zero risk gradient.

- warm-start both recipes from the same frozen wall EMA, sha256
  `cfc618f637f1afb6ba36cb281e895c096288fbc3cbea962fcfc11830513cbeed`,
  selected update 15,500. the optimizer, run step and private training RNG reset.
  this is warm-start refinement, not an independent from-scratch replication.
- both use the existing original corpus and retained 2,080-action supplement,
  identical 19,328 augmented training examples, seed 19, 4,000 updates, batch 64,
  learning rate 1e-4 and best fixed validation denoising loss every 250 updates.
  their only config difference is risk weight 0 versus 256. fractions and all
  margins, other losses, model parameters and draw probabilities are identical.
- refuse a changed original-data, angular-label or geometry identity at warm start.
  require exact checkpoint continuation on the device before training.
- evaluate the untouched anchor, refined control and risk candidate on every
  frozen validation condition, four sampling seeds, selection controls, full zero
  and passive rest, exact pose replay, overlap depth/duration, wall clipping,
  uncertainty strata and the supplementary action horizons. keep every failure.
- one GPU, four CPUs, 24 GB host RAM, 40-minute allocation cap; each training
  process is capped at 15 minutes including label preparation. use immutable
  committed source, preserve failed phases and back up checkpoints separately.
- checkpoint selection does not use penetration scores. reject a candidate that
  trades contact for frozen motion, clipping, drift or collapsed coverage. report
  the matched control rather than substitute the older hardware's control.
- all 248 final reference clips stay reserved. do not clamp, project, rerank,
  discard seeds, change tracker tolerances or pass hidden physics into inference.

outcome and measured costs will be appended after the allocation. passing unit
checks only establishes that this proposed experiment is runnable.
