## Decisions for the call

1. Use a small, versioned dataset-0.3 train/validation pilot for the next shared
   runs. Keep the one-clip dataset-0.1 diagnostic only for controlled debugging.
2. Keep the existing tiny-training gate, and require held-out action response
   before spending on main comparisons. CVAE's training pass does not waive this.
3. Keep the core at 64px: CVAE, matched numeric/spatial diffusion, controls and
   four synchronized futures. Gate 128px, auxiliary losses and extra model
   families on measured motion quality and remaining compute.
4. Assign report assembly and final packaging within the team. Technical owners
   supply their actual methods, results and contributions; these two assembly
   roles are still open.
