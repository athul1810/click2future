# PHOTO-P01: external photo backend feasibility and preview profile

Owner: MAITI SHRIJEET. Registered on 4 November before profile-study generation.
This is a development engineering study, separate from D014-D021 and the
published Force Prompting benchmark. It cannot promote a learned-physics claim.

The official StreamForce download was quota-blocked with gdown 5.2.0 on TC2 and
6.5.1 locally. Jobs 50954/50955 failed during environment setup (index resolution
and home quota); 50956 was stopped before inference after the checkpoint blocker.
The failed attempts and node-local environment remain recorded. They do not
establish that StreamForce is incompatible with A40 hardware.

The fallback source is Force Prompting ef3d0f847f787552b5ce004906c4944292e58996,
using the released point-force checkpoint and CogVideoX-5b-I2V. Job 50957 runs
the README's first ornament example with all four declared seeds 0-3. This is
only a native functional smoke; it is not used to choose the preview profile.
The first sample took 471.48 seconds and has 49 native 720x480 frames at 8 fps.

## fixed profile study

Use the first vase photo in StreamForce's released smoke gallery, point_3.png,
and its fixed caption, point, strength and angle from point_force.csv. The
gallery is public development material; it is not an untouched external test.
Preserve its credit, source revision, file hashes and every generated sample.

The same centre crop is used at both resolutions. Transform the point through
that crop; keep the caption, force angle/magnitude and seeds 0,1,2,3 unchanged.

| profile | size | DPM steps | purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| preview20 | 432x288 | 20 | combined preview candidate |
| native50 | 720x480 | 50 | published-step reference on the same image |
| native20 | 720x480 | 20 | separate step-count effect from resolution |

Run profiles in this declared order. All use the full released generator and
strictly loaded ControlNet, bfloat16, guidance 6, 49 frames and native 8 fps.
Never enable the upstream layer-truncation low-memory hack. For preview20,
bilinearly downsample the native moving-blob condition with antialiasing and
retain the exact preparation in the record. The upstream pipeline does not
resize that condition itself. No video correction, interpolation, solver,
reranking, seed replacement or quality-dependent exclusion is allowed.

Retain all raw tensors and MP4 hashes, timings (including cold caption cost),
memory, input/output shapes and frame-zero reconstruction error. Repeat the
first preview seed after the complete study and report the pixel difference.
The preview replay gate requires byte-exact raw RGB; failure is retained and
does not get relabelled as a successful deterministic run.
Weight/source identity must pass the official artifact checks before the run.
Record startup cost separately; these small-n measurements do not establish a
general latency distribution or physical correctness.

## decision

An executable preview needs all four complete decodable clips, intact input
geometry/subject identity on inspection, no non-finite tensors and a successful
replay record. Inspect every sample at fixed times and retain artifacts. Report
motion/control defects. If subject identity visibly fails, retain the slower
profile; an engineering speed gain cannot establish a quality gain.

Selection is on development material only. A later evaluation panel must freeze
its images/actions/seeds/rating rules before generation. The native scalar's
minimum maps to a nonzero moving blob, so the UI says "minimum", not physical
zero/rest. Equal noise seeds across pushes do not identify a shared world.
